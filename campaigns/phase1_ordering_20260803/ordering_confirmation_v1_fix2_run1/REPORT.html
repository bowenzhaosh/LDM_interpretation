The strongest current diagnosis is proposal degeneracy in the causal oracle.

What may be said

On the frozen d=4/base fleet and reused panel, the ordering-value manipulation check is positive under C and null under N, and the observed control-subtracted metric becomes more negative from 20k to 120k. The direct 120k causal endpoint did not clear and oracle convergence failed, so this attempt establishes neither PFN ordering use nor an undertraining effect.

Next decisive step

Run an outcome-blind oracle-precision pilot on the fixed 400-row nested panel. Compare the frozen prior-proposal estimator with a proposal that targets the causal posterior, and require all existing convergence gates to pass before starting a fresh-seed confirmation. Merely doubling the same proposal is not justified by ESS values near one.
